# LeadScout Ranking Rubric

Only people with **at least one verified public contact channel** are scored. A person with no
confirmable public channel is moved to the **Identified, No Public Channel** section *before* scoring
and does not appear in the ranked list. People matching an opt-out rule or a "not looking" signal, and
any agency or company entry, are excluded entirely. There is no fixed cap — score and rank every
person who passes the gate.

Each verified person is scored out of 100:

| Dimension | Points | Evaluation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Relevance and fit to goal | 35 | Alignment with the user's goal, field, target companies/communities, and the right bucket. |
| Reachability and channel quality | 25 | Strength of the public channel (see ladder below): a person-authored "email/DM me" invite or a published personal email ranks above a bare profile URL. |
| Influence and ability to act | 20 | Whether this person can act on the outreach — a recruiter, hiring manager, or freelance buyer for a job/client goal; a well-connected peer for a network goal. |
| Freshness of signal | 15 | Recency of the post, listing, or activity that surfaced them. Most decisive for freelance and "who's hiring" leads. |

## Channel quality ladder (drives the Reachability score)

From strongest to weakest:

1. A person-authored post that explicitly invites contact with a method ("email me at …", "DM me").
2. A public email the person lists on a page they control (personal site, GitHub profile, conference bio).
3. A public open-DM channel (X with open DMs, a public contact form).
4. A public LinkedIn / X **profile URL** with no published email — reachable only through the platform.

## Handling unknowns

When a contact detail is simply not public (commonly: no published email exists), record it as
**none found** rather than guessing or inventing one. A missing email is not a penalty by itself — the
person is scored on whatever public channel they do have. A person with *no* public channel at all is
not scored; they go to **Identified, No Public Channel**.

## Scoring discipline

- Award points only for evidence actually present on a source page you opened, not assumptions.
- Record a short rationale per dimension when it materially affects the total.
- The Influence score depends on the user's goal — weight differently for a job/client goal vs a
  networking goal, and say which you applied.
- Two people may tie; preserve the rubric order and note ties if relevant.
- A higher freshness score requires a recent, dated signal (a recent post or clearly active page).
